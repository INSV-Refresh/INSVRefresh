# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Salesforce Lightning support agents. Monitor call queues during active work shifts. Use the extension embedded in the browser while working in Salesforce — focused, task-driven context, often in a noisy environment. Premium users pay monthly via ExtPay/Stripe.

## Product Purpose

Chrome extension that monitors Salesforce Lightning queues and notifies agents of new tickets. Auto-refreshes queue pages, plays custom audio alerts, tracks status changes. Frees agents from manual refreshing so they can focus on case resolution. Success = fewer missed tickets, faster response time.

## Brand Personality

Sharp, reliable, unobtrusive. The tool disappears into the work. Moments of warmth (spark coral on the logo) without being playful at the cost of trust.

## Anti-references

- Flashy SaaS marketing aesthetics (gradient blobs, hero metrics, glassmorphism)
- Bloated dashboard chrome (heavy sidebars, nested navigation for simple settings)
- Extension UIs that feel like toy apps

## Design Principles

1. **Disappear into the work** — the UI earns trust by staying out of the way
2. **Clarity over cleverness** — labels, states, and affordances follow conventions; delight is reserved for moments
3. **Honest hierarchy** — free vs. paid boundaries are visible but not aggressive
4. **Consistent vocabulary** — same component shape, same icon style, same motion energy across every screen

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. Dark mode required (Salesforce agents work in varied lighting). Reduced motion support. Portuguese-first (pt-BR), with English and Spanish i18n.
